Delete your Polarity.io data

Polarity.io · last updated 1 August 2026

In the game — immediate

  1. Open Polarity.io
  2. Tap Settings on the main menu
  3. Scroll to Data & Privacy
  4. Tap Delete data
  5. Confirm on the prompt that appears

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. Polarity.io has no accounts or passwords, so there is nothing to sign in to first.

By email

If you have uninstalled the game, email polarity.io.io@gmail.com from any address and include your friend code if you know it (Leaderboard → Friends). Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Records of purchases made through Google Play Held by Google, not by us. Required for tax and refund purposes and not ours to delete — see your Google Play order history.
Advertising identifier Never stored by us. Reset it in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
Database backups Deleted records disappear from backups within 30 days as the hosting provider's backups rotate.

Game progress saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall.
