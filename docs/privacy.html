Privacy Policy

Polarity.io · last updated 1 August 2026

Polarity.io is a mobile game. You can install it and play without creating an account, signing in, or giving us your name, email address or phone number.

What leaves your device

DataWhyWho receives it
An anonymous account identifier So your progress can be restored if you reinstall or change device. It is generated by the game and is not linked to any personal account. Supabase (database hosting)
Your saved progress — coins, gems, unlocked cosmetics, statistics, settings Cloud save. Supabase
The display name you type and your best score Leaderboards. The name is whatever you choose to enter; you are not asked for a real one. Supabase
Your friend code and the codes of players you add The friends leaderboard. Only stored if you use the friends feature. Supabase
Advertising identifier and ad interaction data To show ads. Collected by Google, not by us. Google AdMob

What does not leave your device

Gameplay analytics stay on your device. The game records events locally for debugging and never transmits them.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or files. The game asks for no runtime permissions beyond vibration.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may use an advertising identifier to show relevant ads and to measure performance. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you are shown Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and your choice is respected.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com.

Children

Polarity.io is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and it will be deleted.

Your control over your data

Inside the game, under Settings → Data & Privacy:

  • Export data writes everything held about you to a file you keep.
  • Delete data erases your cloud save, leaderboard entry and friend links, and cannot be undone.

You can also email the address below and ask for deletion.

Retention

Cloud saves and leaderboard entries are kept while the game is installed and in use. If you delete your data it is removed immediately and not retained in backups beyond the hosting provider's standard rotation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
